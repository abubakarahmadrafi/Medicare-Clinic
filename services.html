Our Services

Comprehensive medical services for your health needs

🫀
Cardiology

Expert heart care by qualified cardiologists.

We offer ECG, stress tests, echocardiography, and full cardiac checkups. Our cardiologists treat all heart conditions with modern equipment.
🦷
Dentistry

Complete dental care from cleaning to surgery.

Services include tooth cleaning, fillings, extractions, braces, root canal treatment, and dental implants by expert dentists.
🩺
General Medicine

Routine checkups and treatment for common illnesses.

Covers fever, flu, infections, diabetes management, blood pressure monitoring, and general health checkups for all age groups.
👶
Pediatrics

Specialized care for children and infants.

Complete child healthcare including vaccinations, growth monitoring, nutrition guidance, and treatment of childhood diseases.
🦴
Orthopedics

Bone and joint care by expert orthopedic surgeons.

Treatment for fractures, arthritis, back pain, sports injuries, and joint replacement surgeries using latest techniques.
🧠
Neurology

Brain and nervous system care by specialists.

Diagnosis and treatment of migraines, epilepsy, stroke, Parkinson's, and other neurological disorders using advanced diagnostics.
